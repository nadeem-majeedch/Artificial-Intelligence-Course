usable by anyone studying independently, not only by students in a classroom.

## Design philosophy

The course reflects a simple conviction: AI is best learned by deriving an idea, implementing it,
breaking it and then measuring what it actually does.

- **Intuition before formalism.** Every formula is explained in words before symbols.
- **Mathematics where it is needed.** The prerequisite track supplies the working language of
  modern AI instead of assuming it.
- **Runnable explanations.** Algorithms are implemented in Python that can be changed and tested.
- **Honest evaluation.** Labs and projects reward baselines, error analysis and stated limitations.
- **Responsibility as engineering.** Fairness, privacy and security are assessed, not appended as
  closing remarks.

## About the course

The course contains fourteen modules, 89 module lessons, 21 prerequisite lessons, 35 notebooks,
38 laboratories, 22 assignments, 14 quizzes, 14 self-assessment sets, eight module projects and
twelve capstone briefs. It progresses from the foundations of AI through classical methods, machine
learning, deep learning and modern generative and agentic systems.

More about the course design is in [About the Course](docs/about.md). The full lesson inventory and
outcome mapping are in the [Curriculum Map](docs/CURRICULUM-MAP.md).
