# Projects and Capstones

a module project, a scoped capstone-lite and the full capstone with milestones, evaluation and a
defence.

## The capstone in five rules

1. One primary metric tied to the decision, with a baseline.
2. Split the data as deployment splits it — by time, site or patient, not randomly.
3. Report per-class or per-group results with sample sizes.
4. State uncertainty or say the sample cannot distinguish the models.
5. State what the result does **not** support.

!!! warning "Scope"
    The capstone is a research prototype, not a production deployment. A working demonstration
    without a defensible evaluation is not a completed capstone.
