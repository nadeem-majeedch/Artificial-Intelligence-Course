# Licensing Inventory

Audit of what the repository contains, who owns it, and which licence applies.
Companion documents: [LICENSE](LICENSE) (MIT code), [CONTENT-LICENSE.md](CONTENT-LICENSE.md)
(CC BY 4.0 content), [DATASET-LICENSE.md](DATASET-LICENSE.md) (CC0 data),
[THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md](THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md) (excluded material).

Measured at the Phase 26 release freeze: **403 Markdown files, 96 Python files, 36 notebooks,
2 committed `.npz` files, 11 JSON data/card files, 0 image assets.**

**Phase 26 status:** the approved four-layer architecture is implemented: MIT for original code,
CC BY 4.0 for original educational content, CC0 1.0 for original synthetic datasets, and upstream
terms for all third-party material. Upstream model and UCI dataset verification is recorded in
[THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md](THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md); ownership/chain-of-title review remains
a documented owner action in [OWNERSHIP-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md](OWNERSHIP-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md).

| Quotation | Remains third-party; keep attribution and do not relicense |

## Ownership evidence and limits

- Git history shows a single author identity, `Dr. Muhammad Nadeem Majeed` (one commit).
- The repository has no prior licence headers and no contributor agreements in the tree;
  ownership is therefore recorded from authorship, not from a signed chain of title.
- A single-author Git history is evidence of authorship, not conclusive proof of ownership or
  authority to license.
- Complete [OWNERSHIP-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md](OWNERSHIP-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md) before publication.
- This inventory is a technical classification, **not a legal opinion** and not a
  chain-of-title review. Third-party ownership questions are listed in
  THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS §8 and require owner (or counsel) confirmation.
