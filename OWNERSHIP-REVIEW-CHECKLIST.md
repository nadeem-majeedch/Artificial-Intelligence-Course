**Purpose:** give the repository owner a documented, auditable basis for the ownership statements
in [LICENSE](LICENSE), [CONTENT-LICENSE.md](CONTENT-LICENSE.md), [DATASET-LICENSE.md](DATASET-LICENSE.md)
and [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). This is a checklist, not a legal opinion and not evidence that any
item has already been completed.

## 1. Authorship and chain of title

- [ ] Confirm the full legal name to be used in copyright notices and contracts.
- [ ] List every person or organisation that contributed protectable code, prose, diagrams,

- [ ] For each contributor, record whether the contribution was made as an employee, contractor,
  commissioned work, volunteer, or in another capacity.
- [ ] Obtain a signed contributor licence agreement (CLA), copyright assignment, or written

## 2. Licensing authority

- [ ] Confirm the holder has authority to grant MIT for all original source code.
- [ ] Confirm the holder has authority to grant CC BY 4.0 for all original educational content.
- [ ] Confirm the holder has authority to dedicate all original synthetic datasets to CC0 1.0.
- [ ] Confirm that no exclusive licence, publisher agreement, employer policy or funding condition
  conflicts with these grants.
- [ ] Confirm that the MIT, CC BY 4.0 and CC0 notices name the correct holder and year.
- [ ] Confirm that the mixed-licence structure is described consistently in the README, FAQ,
  citation file and inventory.

## 3. Third-party material

- [ ] Review [THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md](THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS.md) and confirm each recorded
  licence and URL is still current at publication.
- [ ] Confirm that no third-party code, dataset, image, diagram, model weight or long quotation has
  been relicensed by the course.
- [ ] Confirm that UCI digits and Iris are described as CC BY 4.0 and are not committed.
- [ ] Confirm that model weights are not committed and that model-card use restrictions are
  acceptable for the course's demonstrations.
- [ ] Confirm the policy for model outputs and short quotations is acceptable, or obtain advice.
- [ ] Confirm that any external dataset named in a capstone brief includes a licence-verification
  instruction for the student.
- [ ] Record any unresolved item as a release consideration rather than inferring permission.

## 4. Dataset ownership

- [ ] Confirm every committed `.npz`, `.csv` or generated sample is produced solely by course code
  from owned or licensed inputs.
- [ ] Confirm every generator has a fixed seed and a documented provenance.
- [ ] Confirm no real personal data, confidential records or scraped third-party text is present in
  a synthetic dataset.
- [ ] Confirm the M13 dataset cards accurately describe intended use, limits and bias mechanisms.
- [ ] Confirm the CC0 dedication is applied only to original synthetic datasets.

## 5. Publication decision

- [ ] Decide whether the repository will be published with the current mixed-licence structure.
- [ ] Record the publication date and the exact commit or tag the owner will publish.
- [ ] Confirm that no DOI, ORCID, version, release date or institutional contact has been invented
  in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).
- [ ] Confirm that the chosen hosting platform renders the licence files and attribution pages.
- [ ] Retain this completed checklist and supporting records.
