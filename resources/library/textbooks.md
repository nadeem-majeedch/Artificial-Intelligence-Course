# Textbooks

Fourteen books, chosen because each is the best available treatment of something the course teaches.
Free editions are marked; a free book you will actually read beats a better paid one you will not.

### Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach, 4th edition
**Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig** · Pearson, 2020
- **URL:** https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/
- **Modules:** M01–M05, M10, M13 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** The field's standard reference and the source of this course's vocabulary. Use it as an encyclopaedia: read the chapter that matches the module you are in, not the book cover to cover.
- **Access:** Purchase; companion site with exercises, figures and code is free.

### Mathematics for Machine Learning
**Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal and Cheng Soon Ong** · Cambridge University Press, 2020
- **URL:** https://mml-book.github.io/
- **Modules:** P02, M05, M06, M07 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** Fills the gap between "I can multiply matrices" and "I can read a gradient derivation", with each mathematical idea motivated by the ML use it serves.
- **Access:** Free PDF from the authors' site; print edition sold by the publisher.

### Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction
**Kevin P. Murphy** · MIT Press, 2022
- **URL:** https://probml.github.io/pml-book/
- **Modules:** M05, M06, M11 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** A modern, unified treatment that connects classical probabilistic models to deep generative models. The best single book to grow into after this course.
- **Access:** Draft chapters free on the book site; published edition sold by MIT Press.

### Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
**Christopher M. Bishop** · Springer, 2006
- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/book/9780387310732
- **Modules:** M05, M06 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** The rigorous Bayesian view of regression, classification and graphical models. Read the chapter behind whichever M06 algorithm you want to understand properly.
- **Access:** Purchase (Springer); widely held by university libraries.

### The Elements of Statistical Learning, 2nd edition
**Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman** · Springer, 2009
- **URL:** https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/
- **Modules:** M06 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** Bias–variance, regularisation, trees and ensembles explained with unusual care. Chapter 7 is the clearest account of model assessment anywhere.
- **Access:** Free PDF from the authors; print edition sold by Springer.

### Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow, 3rd edition
**Aurélien Géron** · O'Reilly, 2022
- **URL:** https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/
- **Modules:** M06, M07, M09 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Practical and well organised, with complete pipelines. Use it alongside the M06 and M07 notebooks for the engineering habits the lessons do not dwell on.
- **Access:** Purchase (O'Reilly); many libraries provide access.

### Deep Learning
**Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio and Aaron Courville** · MIT Press, 2016
- **URL:** https://www.deeplearningbook.org/
- **Modules:** M07 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** Pre-transformer, but its chapters on optimisation, regularisation and convolutional networks remain the clearest treatment of those ideas.
- **Access:** Free HTML edition from the authors; print edition sold by MIT Press.

### Understanding Deep Learning
**Simon J. D. Prince** · MIT Press, 2023
- **URL:** https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/
- **Modules:** M07, M08, M11 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** The most readable rigorous account of modern deep learning, covering transformers and diffusion as well as the basics. The best single book for M07 and M11.
- **Access:** Free PDF from the author; print edition sold by MIT Press.

### Dive into Deep Learning
**Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li and Alexander J. Smola** · online, continuously updated
- **URL:** https://d2l.ai/
- **Modules:** M07, M08, M09 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** An executable book: every concept arrives with runnable code in PyTorch, NumPy or JAX. Useful when you want to see a technique implemented as well as explained.
- **Access:** Free to read online; the project states its reuse terms on the site.

### Speech and Language Processing, 3rd edition draft
**Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin** · draft, online
- **URL:** https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/
- **Modules:** M08, M11 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** The standard NLP reference, updated to cover contextual embeddings and language models. Chapters 2–8 map directly onto M08.
- **Access:** Free draft chapters from Stanford; a print edition is in preparation.

### Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd edition
**Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto** · MIT Press, 2018
- **URL:** http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html
- **Modules:** M10 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** The definitive text and unusually well written; M10 follows its structure closely. Read chapters 3–6 for tabular methods and 9–13 for approximation.
- **Access:** Free PDF from the authors; print edition sold by MIT Press.

### Interpretable Machine Learning, 2nd edition
**Christoph Molnar** · online, 2022
- **URL:** https://christophm.github.io/interpretable-ml-book/
- **Modules:** M13, M14 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** The practical reference for explanation methods — permutation importance, partial dependence, SHAP, LIME, surrogates — with their failure modes stated.
- **Access:** Free to read online; the author sells a print edition.

### Think Python, 3rd edition
**Allen B. Downey** · O'Reilly, 2024
- **URL:** https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/
- **Modules:** P01 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** The gentlest serious introduction to programming, written for people who have never coded. Use it if the prerequisite material moves too fast.
- **Access:** Free online; print edition sold by O'Reilly.

### Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans
**Melanie Mitchell** · Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2019
- **URL:** https://melaniemitchell.me/aibook/
- **Modules:** M01, M13 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** A clear, sceptical tour of what AI can and cannot do. The best book to read before the course if you want the big picture first.
- **Access:** Purchase; widely held by public libraries.
