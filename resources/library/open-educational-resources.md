# Open Educational Resources

University courses, free curricula and structured tutorials. These are the best free substitutes for
a lecture course, and the best second explanations when a lesson did not land.

[Library index](../README.md) · [Textbooks](textbooks.md) · [Documentation](official-documentation.md)

### 6.034 Artificial Intelligence (MIT OpenCourseWare)
**MIT OpenCourseWare** · 2010
- **URL:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-034-artificial-intelligence-fall-2010/
- **Modules:** M02–M06 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** A complete classical-AI course — search, constraints, learning, probability — with lecture videos, problem sets and exams. Use the problem sets as extra practice for M03–M06.
- **Access:** Free; OCW materials are published under Creative Commons licences (individual items state theirs).

### CS229 Machine Learning (Stanford)
**Stanford University** · course site, notes updated per offering
- **URL:** https://cs229.stanford.edu/
- **Modules:** M06, M07 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** The mathematical version of M06: derivations for least squares, logistic regression, SVMs, bias–variance and learning theory. The lecture notes are the durable part.
- **Access:** Free course notes and problem sets on the site.

### CS231n: Deep Learning for Computer Vision (Stanford)
**Stanford University** · course site with public notes
- **URL:** https://cs231n.stanford.edu/
- **Modules:** M07, M09 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** The clearest free treatment of convolutions, training dynamics and detection/segmentation. Its assignment on writing a CNN from scratch pairs well with PA-07.
- **Access:** Free notes, slides and assignment specifications on the site.

### CS224n: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning (Stanford)
**Stanford University** · course site with public materials
- **URL:** https://cs224n.stanford.edu/
- **Modules:** M08, M11 · **Level:** Advanced
- **Why:** Word vectors through transformers and fine-tuning, taught with the assignments that make the ideas concrete. Good follow-up to M08's transformer lesson.
- **Access:** Free lecture materials and assignment specs.

### Practical Deep Learning for Coders (fast.ai)
**fast.ai** · updated editions
- **URL:** https://course.fast.ai/
- **Modules:** M07, M09, M11 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Top-down teaching: you train a working model in lesson one, then learn why it works. The fastest route from "I know Python" to "I have trained something".
- **Access:** Free to follow online; notebooks published as open-source code.

### Machine Learning Crash Course (Google)
**Google** · self-paced
- **URL:** https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course
- **Modules:** M06 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Short, well-illustrated modules on regression, classification, generalisation and feature engineering, with interactive exercises. Useful revision before the M06 quiz.
- **Access:** Free to use on the site.

### LLM Course (Hugging Face)
**Hugging Face** · continuously updated
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/learn
- **Modules:** M08, M11, M12 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** A structured, free course on transformers, tokenisers, fine-tuning and sharing models, written by the people who maintain the libraries. The most practical companion to M11.
- **Access:** Free to follow; course code is open source.

### Kaggle Learn
**Kaggle** · short tracks
- **URL:** https://www.kaggle.com/learn
- **Modules:** P01, P04, M06 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Short, hands-on tracks for Python, pandas, feature engineering and intro ML, each ending in an exercise you can run in the browser. Good for building fluency fast.
- **Access:** Free with a Kaggle account.

### Spinning Up in Deep RL (OpenAI)
**OpenAI** · 2018, still maintained as a reference
- **URL:** https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/
- **Modules:** M10 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** Explains the RL algorithm families (VPG, TRPO, PPO, DDPG, TD3, SAC) with runnable reference implementations and a glossary that makes the vocabulary stick.
- **Access:** Free; code released under MIT.

### Reinforcement Learning course (David Silver, UCL / DeepMind)
**David Silver** · lecture series
- **URL:** https://www.davidsilver.uk/teaching/
- **Modules:** M03, M10 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** Ten lectures that build from MDPs to policy gradients and model-based planning, with slides and videos. The clearest bridge between search and learning.
- **Access:** Free slides and videos linked from the course page.

### Elements of AI
**University of Helsinki and Reaktor** · online course
- **URL:** https://course.elementsofai.com/
- **Modules:** M01 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** A non-technical introduction to what AI is, what it is not, and what it implies socially. Good preparation for M01 if the terminology feels abstract.
- **Access:** Free to complete online.

### AI for Beginners (Microsoft)
**Microsoft** · open-source curriculum on GitHub
- **URL:** https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners
- **Modules:** M01–M05 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Twelve weeks of lessons, notebooks and quizzes covering symbolic AI through neural networks, with a gentle pace and lots of diagrams.
- **Access:** Open source on GitHub under the repository's stated licence.

### Machine Learning for Beginners (Microsoft)
**Microsoft** · open-source curriculum on GitHub
- **URL:** https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners
- **Modules:** M06 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** A project-based introduction to classical ML — regression, classification, clustering — with scikit-learn throughout. Good extra practice before PA-06.
- **Access:** Open source on GitHub under the repository's stated licence.

### Neural Networks series (3Blue1Brown)
**Grant Sanderson** · video series
- **URL:** https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks
- **Modules:** M07 · **Level:** Beginner
- **Why:** Visual explanations of gradient descent, backpropagation and attention that make the algebra feel inevitable. Watch before L07-03 if the chain rule feels opaque.
- **Access:** Free to watch on the site and YouTube.

### Neural Networks: Zero to Hero (Andrej Karpathy)
**Andrej Karpathy** · video series with code
- **URL:** https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html
- **Modules:** M07, M11 · **Level:** Intermediate
- **Why:** Builds backpropagation, a small language model and a GPT from scratch, line by line. The best free companion to PA-07 and the M11 transformer material.
- **Access:** Free videos; code published as open-source repositories.
