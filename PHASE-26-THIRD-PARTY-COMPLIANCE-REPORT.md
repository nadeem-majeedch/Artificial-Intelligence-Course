## 4. Model outputs

The course's policy is now explicit:

- outputs from pretrained models are **not relicensed** by MIT, CC BY 4.0 or CC0;
- the course makes no ownership claim over them;
- `gen_samples.npz` is generated by VAE/GAN/DDPM models trained from scratch on course synthetic
  digits, so it is treated as original generated data under CC0;
- notebook outputs from pretrained checkpoints are short runtime illustrations, not course-authored
  prose;
- no long verbatim model extraction is intentionally committed;
- a reader reusing an output is responsible for the model terms and any third-party material it may
  reproduce.

The output policy is not a legal opinion. A legal reviewer may reach a different conclusion for a
particular jurisdiction or use.

## 5. Quotations and bibliographic material

The repository stores no paper PDFs, figures or long excerpts. The reference library contains 95
bibliographic entries, cited and linked rather than reproduced. Short quotations used for teaching
or criticism retain their authors' rights.

**Policy:** use only the shortest passage needed to identify or criticise a source; keep author,
work and link attached; do not reproduce a substantial passage, table, figure or code as a
substitute for the original. Any quotation longer than a sentence should be reviewed by the owner
before publication. The Phase 23–26 audits sampled lessons but did not perform a line-by-line
copyright review.

## 7. Attribution obligations

Redistributors must:

1. retain the MIT notice with code;
2. retain the CC BY 4.0 author, title, source, licence and change indication with content;
3. not apply the course licences to third-party material;
4. retain dataset citations and comply with current dataset terms;
5. check model-card terms before redistributing weights or fine-tunes.

These obligations are stated in the README, the three licence documents and
THIRD-PARTY-ATTRIBUTIONS §7.
