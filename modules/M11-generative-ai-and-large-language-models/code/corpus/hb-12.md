# Attendance and leave of absence

Attendance at timetabled sessions is recorded and students on a student visa must attend at least 80 percent of scheduled contact hours to comply with sponsorship conditions. Absence through illness of more than seven days needs a medical certificate. A leave of absence may be granted for one academic year in exceptional circumstances, and the request must be made before the end of the second teaching week. Returning students re-enter at the same stage of the programme.
