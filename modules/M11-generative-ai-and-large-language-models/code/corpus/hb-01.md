# Library opening hours

The Hartley Library on the Northfield campus is open from 08:00 to 23:00 Monday to Friday during term time, and from 10:00 to 18:00 at weekends. During the summer vacation the library closes at 20:00 on weekdays and is closed on Sundays. The 24-hour study centre on level two stays open all night, every day of the year, and is accessible with a valid student card. Group study rooms can be booked online up to seven days in advance, for a maximum of three hours per booking.
