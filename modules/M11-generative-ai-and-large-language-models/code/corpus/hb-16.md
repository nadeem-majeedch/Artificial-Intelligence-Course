# Clubs, societies and sports

The students' union recognises more than 140 societies. Membership fees are set by each society and typically range from 3 to 25 GBP per year. The sports centre offers a student membership of 180 GBP per year or 6 GBP per visit, and includes the swimming pool, gym and courts. Club transport to away fixtures is subsidised by 50 percent. New societies need 15 registered members and a constitution approved by the union, and can apply for a start-up grant of up to 150 GBP.
