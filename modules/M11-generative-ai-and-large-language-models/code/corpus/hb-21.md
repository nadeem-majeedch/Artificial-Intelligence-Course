# Printing, copying and study spaces

Students receive a printing credit of 5 GBP per term, which covers approximately 250 black-and-white pages. Additional credit is purchased online at 0.02 GBP per page for black and white and 0.08 GBP for colour. Printers are located on every floor of the library and in the Sinclair Building. Silent study floors are levels three and four; group discussion is permitted on level two. Poster printing is handled by the media centre and requires three working days' notice.
