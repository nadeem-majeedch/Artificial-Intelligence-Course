# Assessment, marking and grade bands

Undergraduate modules are marked on a percentage scale: 70 and above is a first, 60 to 69 an upper second, 50 to 59 a lower second, 40 to 49 a third, and below 40 a fail. Postgraduate modules require 50 percent to pass and 70 for a distinction. Marks are provisional until the examination board meets in June. Coursework is returned within 15 working days of the deadline. A module mark is capped at 40 when it is passed on a resit.
