# Extensions, late submission and mitigating circumstances

Coursework submitted after the published deadline loses 10 percent of the available marks per working day, up to a maximum of five days, after which it scores zero. Self-certified extensions of up to seven days are available once per academic year for eligible modules. Longer extensions require evidence and the module leader's approval. Mitigating circumstances are submitted to the faculty office, not to the module leader, within five working days of the assessment.
