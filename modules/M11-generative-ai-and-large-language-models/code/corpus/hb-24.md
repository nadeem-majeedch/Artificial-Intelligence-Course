# Religious observance and diversity

The multi-faith chaplaincy in the Old Chapel provides prayer rooms, quiet space and confidential conversation for students of any religion or none. Prayer facilities are open from 07:00 to 22:00 and include washing areas. Students may request timetable adjustments for religious festivals by contacting the faculty office at least 14 days in advance. The equality and diversity team advises on harassment and discrimination complaints, which can also be reported anonymously through the online reporting tool.
