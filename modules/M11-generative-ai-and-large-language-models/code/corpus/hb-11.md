# Academic integrity and misconduct

All submitted work must be the student's own. Contract cheating, including paying another person to write an assignment, is a category one offence and normally results in exclusion from the programme. Unacknowledged use of another author's words or ideas is plagiarism, whether the source is a book, a web page or a generative artificial intelligence tool. Using an AI tool to produce assessed work without permission is treated as misconduct. The university's similarity checking service compares submissions against a database of previous work.
