# Tuition fees and payment deadlines

Undergraduate tuition for home students is 9,250 GBP per year. International undergraduate tuition is 21,400 GBP per year, and postgraduate taught tuition is 24,900 GBP per year. Fees may be paid in full at enrolment or in three instalments due on 15 October, 15 January and 15 April. A late payment charge of 1.5 percent per month applies to unpaid instalments after 14 days. Students experiencing financial difficulty should contact the money advice team before the deadline rather than after it.
