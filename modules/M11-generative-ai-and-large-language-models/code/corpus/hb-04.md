# Wi-Fi and network access

The campus wireless network is called Northfield-Secure and requires your student account credentials. Eduroam is available in all teaching buildings. Wired connections in halls of residence are provided by the accommodation provider, not by the university, and faults should be reported to the hall reception. Streaming services are rate-limited between 18:00 and midnight on the shared network. Virtual private network access is required for library databases from off campus, and the client is available for Windows, macOS and Linux.
