# Accommodation fees and contracts

University-managed halls cost between 128 and 176 GBP per week including utilities and internet. Contracts run for 44 weeks for undergraduates and 51 weeks for postgraduates. A deposit of 400 GBP is required to secure a room and is refunded within 28 days of the end of the contract, less any damage charges. Cancellation is free within 14 days of accepting the offer. Private rental accommodation is not covered by the university contract and disputes are handled by the students' union advice service.
