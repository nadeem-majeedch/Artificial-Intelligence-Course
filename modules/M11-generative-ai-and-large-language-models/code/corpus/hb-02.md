# Book borrowing and renewals

Undergraduates may borrow up to 12 items at a time, postgraduates up to 25, and staff up to 40. Standard loans last 21 days and renew automatically up to three times unless another reader has reserved the item. Reserved items must be returned within seven days. The daily overdue charge is 0.40 GBP per item, capped at 15 GBP per item. Items borrowed through the inter-library loan service are held for collection for ten days and cannot be renewed.
