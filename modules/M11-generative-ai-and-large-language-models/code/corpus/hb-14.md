# Health services and emergencies

The campus health centre is a general practice surgery and requires registration; it is open 08:30 to 18:00 on weekdays. For emergencies call 999. The campus security team can be reached on the internal extension 2222 at any hour and can summon an ambulance. A defibrillator is located in the entrance of every teaching building. International students who pay the immigration health surcharge are entitled to use National Health Service treatment on the same basis as residents.
