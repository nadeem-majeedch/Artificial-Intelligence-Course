# Complaints and academic appeals

A complaint about a service should first be raised informally with the relevant department. A formal complaint must be submitted within 20 working days using the online form. Academic appeals are limited to procedural irregularity, new evidence not available at the time, or bias; disagreeing with the academic judgement of an examiner is not grounds for appeal. Appeals must be lodged within 15 working days of the publication of results, and the outcome is normally issued within 30 working days.
