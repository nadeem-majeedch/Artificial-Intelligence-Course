# Parking and campus transport

Student parking permits cost 120 GBP per year and are allocated by lottery because demand exceeds supply; students living within two miles of campus are not eligible. Visitors pay 1.60 GBP per hour in the pay-and-display car park. The campus shuttle runs every 15 minutes from 07:00 to 22:00 between the main site and the halls. Bicycle storage is free and covered, and a repair stand is available outside the sports centre. Parking fines are 40 GBP, reduced to 20 GBP if paid within 14 days.
