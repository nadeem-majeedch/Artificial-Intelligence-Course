# IT helpdesk and account support

The IT helpdesk in the Sinclair Building answers calls from 07:30 to 20:00 Monday to Friday and from 09:00 to 16:00 on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Password resets can be completed online through the self-service portal at any time. Hardware faults are logged as tickets and the target response is one working day for staff and two working days for students. Lost student cards are replaced at the helpdesk for a fee of 12 GBP, and the replacement is printed while you wait.
