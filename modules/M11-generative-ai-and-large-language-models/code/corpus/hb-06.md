# Scholarships and bursaries

The Northfield Merit Scholarship pays 3,000 GBP per year to entrants with three A grades at A level or equivalent, and is renewed automatically if the student averages 65 percent or higher. The Hardship Bursary is means-tested and awards between 500 and 2,500 GBP per year; applications open on 1 September and close on 31 October. Care-leaver support covers accommodation costs in full for the first year. Scholarships are not repayable and do not affect eligibility for a student loan.
