# International student office and visas

The international office in the Ashworth Building advises on visas, immigration and working restrictions. Students on a student visa may work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full time in the vacations. Registration with the police is required for nationals of certain countries and must be completed within seven days of arrival. The office issues attendance letters for visa applications within three working days. Visa advice is free; the office cannot give legal advice on immigration appeals.
