# Examination rules

Students must arrive 15 minutes before an examination starts. Admission after 30 minutes past the start time is refused. Only transparent water bottles and approved calculators may be taken to the desk; bags are left at the back of the hall. Mobile phones must be switched off and stored, not merely silenced. A candidate who is ill should submit a mitigating circumstances form within five working days with medical evidence, rather than sit the examination and appeal the mark afterwards.
