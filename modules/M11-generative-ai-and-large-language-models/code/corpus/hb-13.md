# Student support and counselling

The wellbeing service offers free, confidential counselling and is open from 09:00 to 17:00 Monday to Friday in the Lawson Centre. Appointments can be booked online or by telephone, and urgent same-day appointments are available each morning. The service does not provide crisis care outside office hours; the emergency number for the campus security team operates continuously. Disability support arranges adjustments such as extra time in examinations, and applications should be made at least six weeks before the assessment.
