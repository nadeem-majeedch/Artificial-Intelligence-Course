# Careers service and placements

The careers service runs drop-in sessions from 10:00 to 16:00 on weekdays and offers one-to-one appointments with a careers adviser. Placement years are arranged through the faculty placement office and must be approved before the end of the second year. Students on a placement year pay a reduced tuition fee of 1,850 GBP. The service reviews applications and runs mock interviews, but does not guarantee an interview or a placement.
