## Key concepts checklist

- [ ] Define demographic parity, equal opportunity and predictive parity
- [ ] Compute selection rates, gaps and the disparate impact ratio
- [ ] Explain why fairness definitions can conflict
- [ ] Distinguish a global explanation from a local one
- [ ] State the efficiency property of Shapley values
- [ ] Describe membership inference and differential privacy
- [ ] Explain an evasion attack and a poisoning attack
- [ ] Name the sections of a model card

## Knowledge-check questions

1. Two groups have selection rates 0.50 and 0.30. Compute the DI ratio and interpret it.
2. Why does reweighing not fix label bias?
3. Why is an attribution not automatically a reason for a decision?
4. What does an $\varepsilon$ budget mean in differential privacy?
5. Why must a negative security test be reported?

## Practical competency checklist

- [ ] Produce a group metrics table with sample sizes
- [ ] Evaluate an explanation for fidelity or stability
- [ ] Run a membership-inference test and interpret the AUC
- [ ] Write a model card whose numbers are traceable

## Suggested revision topics

Fairness definitions and measurement · mitigation and its costs · attribution methods · privacy
attacks and DP · adversarial examples · governance artefacts and audit trails.
